About

What is Vertex?

Vertex (formerly "Sustainably Developing Code") is a 2-week hybrid innovation challenge from the Nexus Innovation Guild and the Meridian Tech Society. It challenges student builders to ship real solutions aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals — not just slides.

Introducing NOVA!

Still have questions?

Vertex is a hybrid innovation challenge where participants develop tech-based solutions that address real-world problems aligned with the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

The challenge is open to foundation, diploma, and undergraduate students from all universities in Malaysia. International students are warmly welcomed — eligibility is based on where you study, not your nationality.

Students from non-technical majors are also encouraged to join! As long as you have enthusiasm for building and a willingness to present your ideas, you are welcome.

Yes. Participants may join individually, as the team size is 1 to 5 members per team.

The registration fee is $15 + a $10 commitment deposit (refundable) per team. The deposit is returned to your team after the event.

The official problem statement will be revealed online on 15 March 2027, 08:00:00 PM MYT (UTC+8).
