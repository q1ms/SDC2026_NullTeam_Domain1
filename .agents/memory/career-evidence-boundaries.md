---
name: Career evidence boundaries
description: Product trust constraints for career recommendations and adoption comparisons.
---

Career recommendations must distinguish source facts, editorial role/skill mappings and hypothetical adoption assumptions. Scores are estimates, never measured probabilities or objective forecasts. News mentions do not measure job demand; always preserve source scope, age and limitations.

**Why:** The product brief explicitly requires a defensible evidence-to-recommendation chain and user control over career decisions, not confident AI-generated forecasts.

**How to apply:** When changing signals, models or recommendation copy, disclose which claims come from a source and which are the app's assumptions. Keep a live-service-independent demonstration and never describe exploration-plan completion as guaranteed employability.

Rich professional profiles remain self-reported. Use explicit skill labels and learning evidence transparently, not narrative claims, institution prestige, GPA or test scores as inferred qualifications.

**Why:** Detailed onboarding serves students as well as experienced workers; collecting more personal information must not create unsupported credential verification or prestige-based scoring.

**How to apply:** Attribute any professional-background skill match to its entered field, distinguish context from scoring, and never use a saved location as evidence of local hiring demand.
