---
name: Scenario data provenance
description: Honesty constraints for default scenario projections and imported job data.
---

Treat the built-in job-growth and resilience values as illustrative hackathon scenario data, not verified labor-market forecasts. Imported datasets must retain their own projection values, including when none of the roles decline.

**Why:** The product demonstrates AI-disruption uncertainty; its bundled projections have no verified external forecasting source. Visual drama must not be presented as real-world certainty or override supplied data.

**How to apply:** Keep illustrative-scenario labeling visible and derive emerging/declining classifications from the actual projected growth rather than fabricating categories for a more dramatic graph.
